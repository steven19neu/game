Rooftop Leap

Touch and drag right to charge power (drag left to reduce), release to jump.

  • Drag sideways: the longer the drag, the more power. You can still charge in mid-air for the next jump.
  • Landing on a roof launches you again immediately. Not touching the screen at that moment means game over.
  • Clip a wall and you'll bounce off it — recover if you land on a roof, or fall into the gap and it's game over.
